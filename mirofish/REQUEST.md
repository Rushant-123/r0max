# MiroFish prediction request

Upload SEED.md as the seed material, then paste this as the prediction request.

---

Build a parallel digital world of one country, 80 million people, zero prior immunity to the virus described in the seed material.

Then introduce the virus ("monster": spread number 8, kill rate 3.5%, spreads before symptoms, reinfects about every 80 days) into this country on day zero, and simulate 500 days.

Key question: in the seed material's equation model, this virus takes over 98.4% of the country even through lockdowns, masks, and a 75% vaccine. Does that still happen in a world of real people?

Specifically watch:

1. Spread: total share ever infected, speed, second waves from reinfection
2. Compliance: do lockdowns hold, or do people rebel? Does compliance fall in the second wave?
3. Information: rumors, panic, migration out of cities
4. Economy: supply chains, school closures, jobs, under a months-long outbreak
5. Politics: leaders trading the economy against health measures, elections, protest waves
6. The end state on day 500: who is infected, who recovered, what the country looks like

Compare against the control: the same country, same countermeasures, but the parent strain (spread number 4, kill rate 2%). The equation model contains that one in under 70 days. Does the society model agree?

Deliverables:

- A prediction report: final numbers (spread, deaths, economy, political fallout) plus the three most decisive turning points
- The interactive world, so I can inject variables from a god's-eye view (later: better vaccine, earlier detection, closed borders)
